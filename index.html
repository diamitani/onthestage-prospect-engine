What We Are Building: The 5-Step Engine

From raw lead ingestion to warm sales conversations on behalf of frontline reps.

📡

1. Multi-Modal Lead Ingestion

Ingests target organizations via batch CSV uploads (state thespian festival lists, school district rosters) and real-time website visitors deanonymized via RB2B & Qualified.

Eliminates manual spreadsheet entry
👤

2. Contact Discovery & Waterfalls

Clay executes a waterfall search across multiple data sources to identify Drama Teachers, Theatre Directors, and Fine Arts Chairs, verifying emails with 100% deliverability.

Zero bounce rates & LinkedIn hunting
🎭

3. Automated Show & Venue Research

AI scans the school's arts page to identify their upcoming production (e.g. "Into the Woods (Spring 2026, 750 seats)") and their current ticketing method (cash, envelopes, Eventbrite fees).

Pins down exact pain points
✍

4. AI Messaging (On Behalf of SDRs)

Claude 3.5 Sonnet writes a friendly, 3-sentence email using the Problem-Agitate-Solve framework, sent from the assigned SDR's inbox referencing their exact musical.

Sounds 100% human-crafted
🚀

5. Outreach Dispatch & CRM Sync

Logs all show intelligence into HubSpot/Salesforce and automatically enrolls the contact into an outbound sequence. The SDR only steps in when the prospect replies.

Reps focus only on warm replies
🛡

+ CRM Deduplication Shield

Checks HubSpot & Salesforce first. If a school is an active client or has an open deal with an AE, they are excluded instantly to protect brand reputation.

Guarantees zero client double-touches

Production Architecture

Prospect Automation Engine — Master Workflow

01
Schedule Trigger & Webhook Intake 01 Daily Schedule / Webhook Intake

The Starter (Intake & Trigger)

How it works: Runs on a daily schedule (e.g. 2:00 AM) to process fresh outbound batches, or triggers on-demand via webhook when an anonymous school visitor is identified on OnTheStage.com via RB2B or when a spreadsheet is uploaded.

Cron: Daily Schedule Webhook Intake: /prospect-automation-intake Payload: CSV / Intent Signals
02
Code Transformation 02 Calculate Date Ranges & Normalize Input

The Data Normalizer

How it works: Cleans raw input data, standardizes school and company domain names, removes unwanted characters, and sets ISO timestamps for seamless downstream processing.

Domain Sanitization Lookback Windows Schema Standardization
03
CRM Query (HubSpot / Salesforce API) 03 Query HubSpot Intent & Dedupe Companies

The CRM Deduplication Shield

How it works: Queries HubSpot and Salesforce by domain. If the school is already a paying customer, an open sales opportunity, or was recently contacted, it is automatically excluded to prevent duplicate messaging.

HubSpot Company Search API Domain Matching Customer Protection
04
Enrichment Adapter (Clay / Amplemarket) 04 Universal Data Tool Adapter

The Contact & Persona Finder

How it works: Queries Clay, ZoomInfo, and Apollo to find the specific individuals running the theatre department (Drama Teachers, Theatre Directors, Fine Arts Department Chairs) and validates email deliverability.

Target Titles: Drama Teacher, Theatre Director, Fine Arts Chair Deliverability Verification LinkedIn Resolution
05
Claude 3.5 Sonnet Engine 05 AI Pain Point Research & Copy Generator

The AI Researcher & Copywriter

How it works: Reads the school's theatre department website, extracts upcoming show titles (e.g. "Mamma Mia!" or "Into the Woods"), identifies ticketing pain points, and writes a tailored 3-sentence email on behalf of the SDR using the Problem-Agitate-Solve framework.

Model: Claude 3.5 Sonnet Problem-Agitate-Solve Copy Generates: Email + LinkedIn DM + SDR Phone Hook
06
Conditional Branch 06 Human Approval Gate

The Safety Switch (Human-in-the-Loop)

How it works: Allows running the system in full automation or routing new drafts to Slack for a quick one-click approval by an SDR before anything is dispatched.

Full Auto vs. Human Review Zero Risk of Bad AI Outputs
07
CRM Upsert (HubSpot / Salesforce Contacts API) 07 CRM Upsert

The CRM Record Creator

How it works: Creates or updates Contact and Organization records in HubSpot and Salesforce, logging the teacher's name, email, school, upcoming show title, and AI pain point summary for complete team visibility.

Contact & Company Upsert Saves AI Research to CRM Fields Lead Status: IN_PROGRESS
08
Sequencer Enrollment (HubSpot Sales / Amplemarket) 08 Sequence Enrollment

The Outreach Dispatcher

How it works: Enrolls the verified drama teacher directly into the appropriate sales cadence sent from the assigned SDR's email address, scheduling automatic multi-day follow-up touches.

HubSpot Sales Hub Sequences Multi-Day Cadence Stops Automatically on Reply
09
Slack Webhook Alert 09 Send Approval Review Notification

The Slack Alert

How it works: Sends an alert into the sales team Slack channel showing the school name, teacher details, and drafted message whenever review is needed.

Real-Time Notifications SDR Channel Visibility

Engine: Orchestration Core
