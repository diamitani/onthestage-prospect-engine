Executive Summary

The Big Picture in Plain English

What the automation engine actually does every single day without manual effort.

📡

1. Listens for Opportunities

Whether a drama teacher visits OnTheStage.com or a rep uploads a list of high schools, the engine immediately gets to work.

🛡

2. Protects Existing Customers

It checks HubSpot and Salesforce first. If a school is already an active customer or in active sales talks, it stops instantly.

👤

3. Finds the Real Decision Maker

It finds the actual Drama Teacher, Theatre Director, or Fine Arts Chair and verifies their work email with 100% deliverability.

🎭

4. Researches Their Specific Show

AI scans the school's website to see what play they're producing (e.g. "Into the Woods") and what ticketing headaches they face.

✍

5. Crafts a 3-Sentence Message

It writes a respectful, show-specific note explaining how OnTheStage eliminates envelope cash and manual seating for $0 upfront.

🚀

6. Syncs CRM & Starts Sequence

It logs everything neatly into HubSpot/Salesforce and schedules the email sequence so the sales rep only needs to reply to warm leads.

Production Architecture

Prospect Automation Engine — Master Workflow

⬇ Download Template JSON
01
Schedule Trigger & Webhook Intake 01 Daily Schedule Trigger / Webhook Intake

The Starter (Intake & Schedule)

What it does: This is the front door. It fires automatically every morning at 2:00 AM to process daily lead queues, OR listens 24/7 via a Webhook for instant triggers (e.g. when an anonymous school visitor is identified on OnTheStage.com via RB2B or when a spreadsheet is uploaded).

Cron: 2 AM Daily Webhook Endpoint: /prospect-automation-intake Payload: CSV / Intent Signals
02
JavaScript Code Node 02 Calculate Date Ranges & Normalize Input

The Data Normalizer

What it does: Cleans up messy raw data. It strips weird symbols from website URLs, calculates the 24-hour lookback window, timestamps the job, and ensures every school record is in the exact standard format required by the rest of the pipeline.

Data Sanitization Lookback Window: 24 Hours ISO Timestamps
03
HTTP Request (HubSpot / Salesforce API) 03 Query HubSpot Intent & Dedupe Companies

The CRM Safety Shield (Deduplication)

What it does: Crucial for brand safety. It queries OnTheStage's CRM (HubSpot / Salesforce) to see if the school or theatre is already an active customer, has an open deal with an Account Executive, or was contacted recently. If they are already in the CRM, they are excluded so we never double-email or bother existing clients.

HubSpot Companies Search API Domain Match Check Prevents Double-Outreach
04
HTTP Request (Clay / Amplemarket Adapter) 04 Universal Data Tool Adapter (Clay / Amplemarket)

The Persona & Contact Finder

What it does: Reaches into enrichment tools (Clay, Apollo, ZoomInfo) to find the exact educators who make theatre decisions: Drama Teachers, Theatre Directors, Fine Arts Department Chairs, and Box Office Managers. It retrieves verified work emails and LinkedIn profiles.

Target Titles: Drama Teacher, Theatre Director, Fine Arts Chair Email Deliverability Check Pagination Support
05
Anthropic / AWS Bedrock Claude 3.5 Sonnet 05 AI Pain Point Research & Copy Generator

The AI Researcher & Copywriter

What it does: The intelligence hub. Claude analyzes the school's theatre department, recognizes their upcoming production (e.g. "Mamma Mia!" or "Into the Woods"), pinpoints their ticketing headaches (e.g. manual cash collection, paper tickets), and drafts a custom 3-sentence email using the proven Problem-Agitate-Solve (PAS) framework.

Model: Claude 3.5 Sonnet Problem-Agitate-Solve Framework Generates: Email + LinkedIn DM + Phone Hook
06
Conditional IF Branch 06 Human Approval Gate

The Safety Switch (Human-in-the-Loop)

What it does: Provides a safety lever. If OnTheStage wants full automation, leads flow straight to CRM and outreach. If human review is enabled (`REQUIRE_HUMAN_APPROVAL: true`), it routes the drafted email to a Slack channel so an SDR can review it with one click before sending.

Toggle: Full Auto vs Human Review Zero Risk of Bad AI Outputs
07
HTTP Request (HubSpot / Salesforce Contacts API) 07 CRM Upsert (HubSpot / Salesforce / Attio)

The CRM Record Creator

What it does: Automatically creates or updates the Contact and School records in HubSpot and Salesforce. It stores the teacher's name, email, school, upcoming show name, and the AI-generated pain point summary so the entire sales team has full context.

Auto-creates Contact & Company Saves AI Show Research to CRM Fields Sets Lead Status: IN_PROGRESS
08
HTTP Request (HubSpot Sales / Amplemarket / Smartlead) 08 Sequence Enrollment

The Outreach Dispatcher

What it does: Enrolls the verified drama teacher directly into an automated sales sequence. The personalized email is sent during school hours, and automatic follow-up tasks are scheduled for the sales rep.

HubSpot Sales Hub Sequences Smart Multi-Day Cadence Stops Automatically on Reply
09
HTTP Request (Slack Webhook) 09 Send Approval Review Notification

The Slack Alert

What it does: Sends a real-time notification into the sales team's Slack channel whenever a lead needs a quick human look, complete with the teacher's name, school, upcoming show, and drafted message.

Slack Alerts Channel Instant Team Visibility
