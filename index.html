Core Engine Process

How the Engine Works: 6 Steps

From raw lead trigger to outreach dispatch on behalf of your sales reps.

Step 1

Lead Intake & Search

Trigger the workflow in one of two ways:

  • Upload a spreadsheet: Drop in school district lists or state theatre festival registries.
  • NLP ICP Search: Connect to a data tool using a prompt that automatically filters for target criteria (school size, theatre programs, geography).
Step 2

Get Companies & Contacts

The company list is pushed to your tool of choice (Clay, Amplemarket, ZoomInfo, Apollo, etc.) with pre-set ICP filters to pull verified contacts:

  • Drama Teachers & Theatre Directors
  • Fine Arts Department Chairs
  • Community Theatre Artistic & Managing Directors
Step 3

Enrichment & Web Research

The engine runs automated web research on the school/theatre and the prospect to gather live context:

  • Upcoming productions & show dates (e.g. Spring musical)
  • Auditorium / venue size and seating structure
  • Current ticketing setup (cash at door, paper tickets, generic ticketing platforms)
Step 4

Pain Point Summary

The system synthesizes the research into a clear pain point hypothesis:

  • Example: "East High School is running 'Into the Woods' in April in a 750-seat hall, currently using envelope cash with zero online reserved seating and losing 20+ hours a week on box office admin."
Step 5

Custom AI Messaging Generation

Claude drafts natural, personalized outreach based directly on the identified pain point, written on behalf of the assigned SDR:

  • 3-Sentence Cold Email: References their specific show and explains how OnTheStage solves their ticketing headache at $0 upfront cost.
  • LinkedIn Message & SDR Call Hook for multi-channel follow-up.
Step 6

Send to CRM & Outreach Portal

The verified contact, company data, and custom messaging are automatically synced:

  • CRM Sync: Logged cleanly in HubSpot or Salesforce with research notes attached.
  • Outreach Portal: Enrolled into the active sequence (HubSpot Sales, Amplemarket, Smartlead, Outreach) so emails go out automatically from the SDR's inbox.
System Architecture

Prospect Automation Engine Architecture

A unified flow connecting intake, data enrichment, AI research, CRM safety shields, and outreach.

1. Input Layer: Spreadsheet CSVs, website visitor intent (RB2B), and NLP prompt searches.
2. Data Enrichment Adapter: Plugs into Clay, Apollo, ZoomInfo, or Amplemarket to pull verified faculty.
3. AI Reasoning Node (Claude 3.5): Extracts show seasons, identifies ticketing pain, and writes PAS copy.
4. CRM Shield & Sequencer: Deduplicates in HubSpot/Salesforce and dispatches via HubSpot Sales/Amplemarket.

The Data Normalizer

How it works: Cleans raw input data, standardizes school and company domain names, removes unwanted characters, and sets ISO timestamps for seamless downstream processing.

The CRM Deduplication Shield

How it works: Queries HubSpot and Salesforce by domain. If the school is already a paying customer, an open sales opportunity, or was recently contacted, it is automatically excluded to prevent duplicate messaging.

Claude 3.5 Sonnet Engine 05 AI Pain Point Research & Copy Generator

The AI Researcher & Copywriter

How it works: Reads the school's theatre department website, extracts upcoming show titles (e.g. "Mamma Mia!" or "Into the Woods"), identifies ticketing pain points, and writes a tailored 3-sentence email on behalf of the SDR using the Problem-Agitate-Solve framework.
